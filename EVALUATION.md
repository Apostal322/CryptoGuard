## Methodology

- **Ground truth** comes from public incident reports, cross-checked on-chain, and is stored in each case's `manifest.json`. It is used only to score detector output.
- **Detector input** is a fixture recorded once from an RPC provider (`fixture.json`, hash below): all ERC-20 transfers of the wallet in a fixed window, transaction signers, and every wallet-signed transaction (found by nonce bisection, not by searching where the answer is).
- **Point in time**: the detector is evaluated at every block where new data appears before the harmful block, seeing only records at or before that block. This is exhaustive for finding the earliest possible warning.
- **A warning** means rule CG-POI-001 or CG-POI-003 on the address that later received the funds. A finding that appears only once the harmful transfer is visible is reported as post-incident classification, never as early detection.
- **Visibility levels** are evaluated separately: ERC-20 data only, and ERC-20 data plus the wallet's own signed transactions (what the live snapshot pipeline now sees).
